# Meal Planner — Plan 3: Planner Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The week planner: plan entries (recipe or note) per date and slot, the Friday planning screen with
suggestions and tap-tap/drag, closing the week, history, last-eaten statistics, and a phone day view.

**Architecture:** Backend: `plan_api.py` (week, entries, closing, history), `weeks.py` (week arithmetic, pure),
`suggestions_api.py`, and eaten statistics added to recipe summaries via `stats.py`. Entries belong to a **date**,
never to a week; a week is computed from the date and the `week_start` setting. "Eaten" = an entry on a day before
today that isn't marked not eaten. Frontend: `PlanPage` (grid + suggestion panel, tablet), `PlanDay` (phone),
`CloseWeek`, `History`; drag & drop with `@dnd-kit/core` (pointer + touch), tap-tap always available.

**Tech Stack:** as Plans 1–2, plus `@dnd-kit/core` 6.x.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-meal-planner-design.md` — "Week planner", "Closing the week", "History"
**Builds on:** Plan 1 (auth, settings `family_size`/`week_start`, `WEEKDAYS`, e2e helpers), Plan 2 (`Recipe`,
`recipes_api.recipe_out`/`_Votes`/`sort_key`, `RecipeSummary`, `RecipeCard`, `Stars`, `PeopleStepper`, `formatAmount`,
`createRecipe` e2e helper).

## Global Constraints

- Slots, in this order: `breakfast` (Zmorge / Breakfast), `snack` (Znüni & Zvieri / Snacks), `lunch` (Zmittag /
  Lunch), `dinner` (Znacht / Dinner).
- An entry is **either** a recipe (with `people`, default = family size at creation, 0.5–100) **or** a note
  (1–200 chars, e.g. "Pizza-Abend"). A slot holds any number of entries, ordered by `position`.
- Everyone may plan, move, remove, set people, close a week and mark meals not eaten. Parents and children alike.
- **Eaten** = entry with `date < today` (Europe/Zurich, `TZ`) and `eaten = true`. Only recipe entries count for stats.
- Week of a date: starts at `date - ((date.weekday() - week_start) % 7)`, 7 days.
- Default week shown: the week containing **tomorrow** (on the last day of a week — Friday with a Saturday start —
  the planner opens on the coming week).
- Counter: a slot counts as planned if it has any entry (notes included); total = 28.
- Suggestion tabs: `favorites` (anyone's favourite; best average first, unrated last), `long_ago` (eaten before,
  average ≥ 3 or unrated; longest ago first), `new` (never eaten; newest first), `again` (has a not-eaten entry in
  the past and no entry dated after it; most recent first). All exclude deleted recipes; all accept `q` and `tag`
  (same matching as `/api/recipes`).
- Deleted recipes stay visible in plan and history (title shown, marked deleted), but can't be newly planned.
- Error codes (new): `entry_not_found` (404), `recipe_or_note` (422: neither or both given), `recipe_deleted` (400).
- All new visible text in the locales.

## Review Focus

1. **Changing the first day of the week after weeks were planned and closed** → entries stay on their dates; weeks regroup; closed state follows the stored closure ranges, nothing is lost. Test in Task 2.
2. **Today's dinner** (planned, date == today) → not counted as eaten yet, so it doesn't push "Lange her" around before it happened. Test in Task 2.
3. **Tap a recipe, then tap a cell, on an iPad** (no hover, no right-click) → planned; tapping the same card again unselects; tapping a cell with nothing selected offers a note. Test in Task 4 (e2e with `hasTouch`).
4. **A recipe deleted after it was planned** → still shown in the plan and history with its title, can be removed, but the suggestion panel and new plans don't offer it. Test in Task 1 and Task 3.
5. **Two people planning on two devices** → each sees the other's entries after a reload; no entry is overwritten (entries are rows, not a week blob). Test in Task 1 (API).

| `DELETE /plan/entries/{id}` | 204 |

### Task 2: Eaten statistics, closing the week, history

**Files:**
- Create: `backend/app/stats.py`, `tests/test_close_history.py`
- Modify: `backend/app/plan_api.py`, `backend/app/recipes_api.py` (`_Votes.summary` adds stats), `backend/app/schemas.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `stats.eaten_stats(db, recipe_ids: list[int]) -> dict[int, tuple[date | None, int]]` — one grouped query: `max(date)`, `count(*)` over entries with `eaten` and `date < today()`.
  - `RecipeSummary` gains `last_eaten: date | None`, `times_eaten: int` (everywhere summaries appear: list, view, plan entries, suggestions).
  - `POST /plan/close {start: date}` → `WeekOut` with `closed: true`; normalises `start` to its week start; stores `WeekClosure(start, end=start+6)`; idempotent. Closing a week whose last day is ≥ today → 400 `week_not_over`.
  - `WeekOut.closed` = all 7 days of the week lie inside stored closures (`start <= day <= end` for some closure). When `week_start` changes, regrouped weeks that are only partly covered show `closed: false` (honest; nothing lost).
  - `WeekOut.previous_needs_closing: bool` — the week before `start` is over, not closed, and has at least one recipe entry.
  - `GET /plan/history?before=&weeks=8` → `list[WeekOut]`, weeks strictly before `before` (default: the current week's start), newest first, only weeks with entries, at most `weeks` (1..52).
  - `GET /recipes/{id}/history` → `list[date]` eaten dates, newest first.
  - Error code: `week_not_over` (400).
